Scope: the public landing page in 8 languages (web/src/features/landing). Mode: Persuade. Static HTML, no framework JavaScript; one self-hosted motion script.

Audience and job: someone arriving from an invite or a search, deciding whether to trust SettleMint with a group's money. They should leave knowing it is free, exact and fast, and press "Start splitting, free".

## Direction contract

THESIS: The product's own glass and numbers are the spectacle: a liquid-glass lens drifts over a real, localized balance and bends it. The page refuses the SaaS template: hero with a phone mock, a stats strip, a grid of six icon features, numbered steps.

OWN-WORLD: The app's world at poster scale: mint-tinted paper-white canvas, near-black ink, one mint call to action. Display in Mona Sans expanded up to 92px with tight tracking; amounts huge and tabular. The lens is the app's own tab-bar capsule rendered as refractive glass (WebGL2). Below the fold, chapters show real app components at work; facts become an Apple-style spec sheet with hairlines.

STORY: see a balance being bent and settled under glass; understand split anything, exact to the cent, any currency, get paid in a tap; believe it from the product doing it, not from claims; start free.

FIRST VIEWPORT: desktop. Floating glass header capsule centred at the top. Left five columns: two-line headline at 88px, a 19px lead, a mint "Start splitting, free" button and a "How it works" link. Right seven columns: the stage, a poster of "You're owed / €111.50" in giant expanded numerals with the lens drifting over it. Phone: headline, lead and buttons first, then the stage.

FORM: Apple product-page storytelling (a sticky stage that changes scene with the scroll). Signature interaction: the lens; then three scroll chapters (split three ways, a currency converts, settle-up ring). seed: none (pinned) "Cinematic, still fast (Recommended): A live 3D/liquid hero and a scroll-driven product story: the real app animating through a split, a currency change and a settle-up as you scroll."

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
